## Verified but cut for relevance

- **Huawei Atlas 960E, September 17:** announcement and provider performance claims confirmed. Hardware scale alone does not make a sufficiently specific connection to one of the obligations. Cut rather than turning the opening into a general AI news feed.
- **Census, September 24 revision:** article originally August 11, reporting a March survey. The explicit note says figures were revised September 24 using updated weights, while the footer says September 23. The note is the basis for the revision date, and both dates would need disclosure if used. Self-reported time savings do not establish wages or macro productivity. Cut because that methodological revision connects more directly to the broader provision argument than to a specific obligation; no forced recourse mapping.

## Held out for insufficient date precision

- **OpenAI rolling Hugging Face / third-party impact page:** primary text supports ongoing review and dozens notified, but the visible September tab supplies no defensible publication day. No retrieval date substituted.
- **Anthropic economic scenario explorer:** primary text supports conditional scenarios, changed labor/capital shares, and omitted policy responses, business cycles and financial disruption. Version label is September 2026 without a publication day. No day invented. These scenarios are not forecasts.

## Excluded unverified leads

The proposed SAFA body, September 25 government-site incidents and September 26 Axios aggregate claims remain unsubstantiated in this verification pass and are not shipped.

## Presentation decisions

The seven entries use descending publication/event dates. They do not represent complete coverage or imply equal intervals. Each contains visible date-kind, attribution, limitations and an inline obligation explanation. Links to the later native obligation disclosures are enhancements: without JavaScript the summary is reachable and keyboard-operable, while the explanation already travels with the event. No automatic opening is promised.

The timeline is editorially maintained data, not a live feed. Existing Introduction and Provision stay consecutive. Its optional thread drawing uses the existing Renewal scheduler with a separate 1001×700 gate; the existing comparison gate remains 1050×850. Both motion buttons share and display the same paused state.
