# Artwork provenance

All site images are AI-generated conceptual illustrations, not documentary photographs. The original city and street images are described in [CONTEXT.md](CONTEXT.md).

Two additional images were generated using the built-in image-generation tool on 8 September 2026, then converted to WebP at quality 82. Original PNGs were retained outside the repository.

## Infrastructure

Saved as `public/assets/infrastructure.webp` (1536 × 1024), used in the release chapter.

Prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide editorial chapter image for Common Obligations, a visual essay about AI accountability. Generate a cinematic monochrome conceptual photograph of an unbranded data center interior: long rows of black server cabinets receding into depth, cables and precise metallic detail, one far doorway admitting soft white daylight. Human scale, physical infrastructure behind powerful AI, quiet and contemplative rather than dystopian. Wide landscape 3:2 composition suitable for a panoramic center crop, no text overlays. Rich charcoal blacks, silvery highlights, subtle analog grain, restrained realistic architectural photography. No readable text, no logos, no UI overlays, no robots, no neon, no identifiable real facility. This will be labeled an AI-generated conceptual illustration.

## Assembly

Saved as `public/assets/assembly.webp` (1536 × 1024), used in the international coordination chapter.

Prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide editorial chapter image for Common Obligations, a visual essay about international AI coordination. Generate a cinematic monochrome conceptual photograph of an empty circular international meeting chamber, seen from an elevated oblique angle. A broad continuous curved table with many equally sized chairs, papers absent, microphones discreet, tall windows letting soft daylight fall across the room. Architecture expresses the difficult work of gathering many perspectives, not a triumphant summit. No people, national flags, UN insignia, readable text, or logos. Not an identifiable real location. Wide landscape 3:2 composition suited to panoramic cropping. Rich charcoal shadows, silvery light, slightly muted green undertone, subtle analog grain, realistic architectural material detail, elegant and sober. No futuristic holograms or sci-fi. This will be labeled an AI-generated conceptual illustration.
